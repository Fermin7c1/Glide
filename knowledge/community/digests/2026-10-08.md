# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-08 (first scan)

## Leveling-relevant
- **Green mobs give 4–20% less XP than Classic; same-level mobs unchanged.** Blizzard (Zirene, forever-bugs #138) says it's intended, to offset food and campfire rested XP. Glide's own kill data fits the same mob-level formula. Kill mobs at or above your level. `official`, model-impact
- **Dungeon quests: extra XP above normal quest XP cut 50%** (Oct 1 build). Deviate Hides went from ~4,650 to ~3,100 XP (Classic 1,600). Quest gear dropped from blue to green. `official`, model-impact
- **Dungeon mob kill XP also reduced**, no numbers published yet. `corroborated`
- **Beta phase 2: cap 30.** Excavation Site: Wetlands (26–31) is new; Razorfen Downs and Uldaman are open; Hillsbrad and Stonetalon are in; 10 more talent points. The Dalaran dungeon is delayed. `official`
- **Faster respawns** in Hillsbrad, Ashenvale, Wetlands, Duskwood and Thousand Needles. Escort quests no longer fail with 25+ quests in the log. `official`
- **Campfire works in any resting pose**, and you can cast, craft and buff while waiting. No campfire damage below level 5. `official`
- **Blizzard: average 1–20 is 20.5 h on Forever vs 20.4 h on Era.** Forever isn't faster. `official`
- **Druid Wrath base damage +~50%** (Sept 24). `official`
- **Hunter Concussive Shot stun talent at 100% proc** (datamined; not in a patch note). `reported`
