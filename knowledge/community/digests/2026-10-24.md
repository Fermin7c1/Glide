# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-24

The beta closed on Oct 23 (Kaivax, "Thank You For Testing"). Character creation and name reservation open Tue Oct 27, and launch is Nov 4 at 3:00 p.m. PST (23:00 UTC). No patch notes were posted after Oct 15. The last beta build (70532) has the same files as 70450; what changed on it came as server hotfixes, which trackers have now read out. There was no run on Oct 23, so this digest covers Oct 22 10:00 UTC onward.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Launch time is official: Nov 4, 3:00 p.m. PST = 23:00 UTC (00:00 CET Nov 5).** It's one global moment. Blizzard's announcement body says PST (its intro says "PDT", a typo since DST ends Nov 1), and the French article says midnight Paris. Beta characters don't carry over.

  `official` — n:20261009-beta-launch-dates (updated)
- **Levels 30-60 were never tested publicly.** Podcast ep. 5 (Oct 22): the beta stopped at 30 with 4 of the 9 new dungeons, everything above was internal only, and quick post-launch fixes are planned. Expect 30-60 hotfixes in the first weeks, and trust live observations over the seed above 30.
