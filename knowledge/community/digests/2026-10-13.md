# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-13

There were no new official posts again. The dev notes and known issues are still the Oct 8 versions, and the Arctium tracker shows no Forever blue posts since Oct 9. The day's real signal came from the bug tracker. Designer Aidan Moon closed about fifteen issues overnight and said they are fixed in the Oct 15 (Thursday) build. That confirms both the date of the next build and part of its contents. Nobody from Blizzard has answered the forum's dungeon-XP threads. "One simple question people refuse to answer" reached 298 posts and 321 likes in a day.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Oct 15 build fixes, confirmed by the designer.** Each of these fixes ships Oct 15:
  - Siphon Life ticks can crit (#303).
  - Twin Disciplines now boosts Mind Flay (#322) and SW:D (#328).
  - Improved Berserker Rage breaks roots (#305).
  - Malediction raises Hellfire damage to enemies (#311).
  - Frost Channeling no longer adds threat (#324).
  - The Faerie Fire stacking exploit is removed (#308).

  Two reports were closed as not a bug. Hunter Predator's Edge crit damage does not apply to white melee (#304). Tremor Totem stays at 30 yd (#301). `official` — n:20261013-oct15-build-confirmed-fixes
- **Warlock: Soul Link through Talented below 30** is fixed in the Oct 15 build (#302). Until then, don't spend points on it. Now `official` — n:20261012-soul-link-talented-below-30-broken
- **Hunter: Improved Mend Pet cost bug** is fixed in the Oct 15 build (#316). Now `official` — n:20261011-improved-mend-pet-cost-bug
- **Warlock: Succubus melee crits about 0.7%, against a 4.58% tooltip.** That's 12 crits in 1,718 swings at level 30 vs level 30 mobs. Lash of Pain crits 6.3%. Staff skill makes no difference. GitHub #331 has logs, and a second user confirmed it. No designer reply yet. `reported` — n:20261013-warlock-pet-melee-crit-low
- **World Refresh (layer switch).** It moves you to another layer every few minutes, with one player reporting every 3–5 min. It also triggers on group invite, join or leave. Players report landing in layers with more people where the quest mobs were already farmed. Group up before reaching kill areas. `corroborated` (forum Oct 13 + Icy Veins Sept 24) — n:20261013-world-refresh-layer-switch
- **Ding! v1.3.0.** The repo moved to moust4ki/DingGuideWoWForever, and the addon now carries its own data, so QuestieDB is no longer needed. It has 1–60 routes for both factions. The new default route takes quests up to +2 levels and mobs up to +1. It also adds a Cozy Sleeping Bag item guide. `reported` — n:20261012-ding-leveling-addon
- **Leveldon** is a new route addon with a fastest route per race and class for 1–30. It counts the kills a quest needs from Wowhead drop rates and requires the Questdon addon. `reported` — n:20261013-leveldon-route-addon
