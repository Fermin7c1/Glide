# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-12

Still quiet on the official side. Blizzard made no new posts on Oct 10–12: the dev notes and known issues are still the Oct 8 versions, and the leveling build is still 70291. Client 70338 (Oct 10) only added cosmetics. The next build is expected Oct 15. Beta ends Oct 22 at cap 30, and launch is Nov 4. The community is arguing loudly about dungeon XP: "Let's End this Dungeon XP Argument with Real Numbers" has 230 replies and 313 likes in a day. Nobody from Blizzard has answered there.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Dungeon elite XP, measured once.** After the Oct 8 buff, a level 18 in a 5-man got 34–36 XP per Deadmines elite (18–22 at level 16 before the buff). The same group got 50 XP per open-world elite. One run gives about 3–4 XP-bar bubbles of kill XP, roughly 5–7 runs per level at 18 without quests. `reported`, model-impact — n:20261012-deadmines-elite-xp-measured
- **XP/hour claims in the big thread.** Questing is said to give 50–65k XP/h at 35–40, against an estimated 15–20k for spellcleave dungeon grinding after the nerf. The only beta measurement posted is ~16k XP/h questing at 13–15. Calibration only. `reported` — n:20261012-dungeon-vs-quest-xph-claims
- **Warlock: Soul Link taken through Talented does nothing below 30.** It costs mana but applies no buff, reproduced at levels 25–29. Don't spend points on it before 30. `corroborated` (GitHub #302 + EU forums) — n:20261012-soul-link-talented-below-30-broken
- **Group quest drops are round-robin.** Drop chance is unchanged and items aren't shared, so a duo needs about twice the kills on collection quests (Aggrend on X). `official` — n:20261012-group-quest-drop-round-robin
- **Escort NPCs** reappear 5–15 min after the previous escort ends. `reported` (Icy Veins) — n:20261012-escort-respawn-5-15min
- **Zephras Isle (Skyborne start).** Tornadoes give Blessing of Zephras: +40% run speed for 5 min. Damage doesn't break it, but any hostile action does. `corroborated` — n:20261012-blessing-of-zephras-speed
- **RestedXP dungeon guide (WIP) is in their public repo.** The Alliance route is 15–16 Hall of Thanes → 16–18 Ruins of Lordaeron → 18–20 Deadmines → Redridge. Horde has a 12–22 Barrens variant. RXP also now says to build campfires while waiting for boats, not on them. `reported` — n:20261012-restedxp-dungeon-guide-wip
- **Dual spec** unlocks at level 40 for 50g. Greenfield floated level 30 on podcast ep. 3. `official` — n:20261012-dual-spec-level-40-maybe-30
- **Legacy perk Master Chef:** 5 ranks, up to a 50% chance of an extra cooked item. That halves the materials for +5% kill-XP food. `reported` — n:20261012-legacy-master-chef
- **Ding:** a new free open-source 1–60 leveling-guide addon. Its routes are generated from QuestieDB for XP/h, and 30–60 is untested. `reported` — n:20261012-ding-leveling-addon
