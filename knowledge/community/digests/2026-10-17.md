# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-17

No scan ran on Oct 15 or 16, so this one covers Oct 14–17. The big event is the **Oct 15 beta build 1.60.1.70425** and its development notes (post 6 of the dev-notes thread). It has racial reworks, retunes for every class, wands and Auto Shot needing you to stand still, faster Wetlands respawns, and quest fixes in seven zones. A cosmetics-only client build, 70450, followed. The stress test moved to **Oct 21**. Diffing forever-ref's per-build client snapshots turned up changes the notes don't mention.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Wands and Auto Shot need 0.5 s standing still before firing.** This hits every wanding caster and kiting Hunter. The bug-tracker issue was closed Not A Bug citing the notes. `official` — n:20261017-wand-autoshot-stand-still (it supersedes n:20261009-auto-shot-cast-time)
- **Racials reworked:**
  - Human and Orc now get +2% and +1% crit on any weapon.
  - Blood Fury's cooldown goes to 3 min.
  - Troll Regeneration is now 20%.
  - Tauren Plainsrunning adds +3% mounted speed.
  - Skyborne get 3% haste.
  - Undead Touch of the Dead procs 30% for casters and 20% for melee, with a 25 s ICD.

  A bug report says Touch of the Grave misses almost every time (#369). `official` — n:20261017-racials-oct15
- **Wetlands respawns are faster and more even** (Dragonmaw Orcs, gnolls, Incendicite Ore). `official`, model-impact — n:20261017-wetlands-respawns-oct15
- **Quest fixes:**
  - Stormwind's "The Attack!" is no longer an escort and can be shared.
  - Darkshore's "How Big a Threat?" now gives credit at every furbolg camp, "For Love Eternal" can be turned in any time, and "Body and Heart" keeps the moondust.
  - Ashenvale's "Raene's Cleansing" has more Bloodtooth Guards.
  - Teldrassil's Lord Melenas no longer evades.
  - Zephras anchors are unblocked.
  - The Druid Cat Form quest relics were hotfixed on Oct 16 (Kaivax).

  `official` — n:20261017-quest-fixes-oct15, n:20261017-druid-cat-form-quest-hotfix
- **Horde are attacked on sight in Dalaran.** The Sewer Key questline no longer opens the city, though the dungeon is unaffected. `official` — n:20261017-dalaran-horde-hostile
- **Dalaran → Stormwind portal reported missing for Alliance Skyborne since Oct 15** (#380, two reporters). Skyborne leaving Zephras at ~14 face level ~26 mobs on the way to Southshore. `reported` — n:20261014-skyborne-only-dalaran-portals (updated)
- **Mob HP is above Era at 40+.** Examples: a level 46 Highborne Lichling has 3.2k HP vs 2.8k on SoD, and King Bangalash has 7k vs 5.5k on Era. A Blizzard designer closed the report as not planned (#347). `official` (closed without comment), model-impact — n:20261017-mob-hp-above-era
- **Resurrection Sickness is 1 minute at every level from 10 since 70425** (two reports, #25 and #363). It's probably a bug and may change before launch. `corroborated` — n:20261017-res-sickness-1min-after-70425 (it supersedes the 10-minute note)
- **Class retunes that matter for leveling:**
  - Mage: Frost Blast at 24 (40% slow, 1.5 s, 40 mana; ranks at 36 and 48). Ice Lance vs frozen targets is 200%.
  - Shaman: Chain Lightning costs ~35% less. Fire Nova is cut. Lava Burst is learned from 30 as the 21-point talent.
  - Priest: Devouring Plague has a 24 s CD, Vampiric Embrace 30 s, and Improved Mind Flay ramps per tick.
  - Paladin: Consecration's coefficient drops 0.095 → 0.042, and the Seal of Righteousness spell-damage bug is fixed (a nerf).
  - Druid: Shred is learned at 20.
  - Warlock: Shadow Mastery is doubled to 2–10%.
  - Hunter: pets use the higher crit, traps scale with AP, Multi-Shot is instant, and pet abilities are nerfed.
  - Warrior: Overpower works on much lower-level mobs. Stance bars are bugged.

  All `official` — n:20261017-mage-frost-blast-24, -shaman-oct15, -priest-oct15, -paladin-oct15, -druid-oct15, -warlock-oct15, -hunter-oct15, -warrior-oct15
- **Datamined, not in the notes:**
  - Spell 24867 "Feral Swiftness" (learned at 8) changed from +30% to +15% Cat Form speed; the talent text is unchanged. `reported` — n:20261017-feral-swiftness-24867-15pct
  - Portal of Summoning now has a 1 h CD and costs 100% mana.
  - Amplify Curse gives +50% to Curse of Exhaustion.
- **Cooking drinks with +5% kill XP.** The new teas and smoothies (Peace/Royal/Root/Triage Tea, Venomous/Slimy/Calcified Smoothie, Mrrggl Smrrthle) restore mana and give the well-fed +5% kill XP after 10 s of drinking. They're available from level 1, 5, 15 and 25. `corroborated` (client data + Warcraft Tavern) — n:20261017-cooking-drinks-5pct-kill-xp
- **Camp Tent confirmed by a second client extract.** It tops rested XP up to 5% of a level after 30 s, once per hour, and is unchanged through 70450. Upgraded to `corroborated` — n:20261010-camp-tent-rested-5pct
- **Eight starter-zone white drops are now greens** (+1/+2 stat, BoE, levels 3–8). `corroborated` — n:20261017-starter-whites-to-greens. The 16 Desolace centaur rewards now need level 35 (client data) — n:20261009-desolace-centaur-quests-35 (updated)
- **Stress test moved to Oct 21.** It starts at 2 p.m. PDT / 19:00 CEST. The beta goes offline, Forever runs a few hours on launch hardware, then those characters are deleted. `official` — n:20261017-stress-test-oct21 (it supersedes the Oct 19 note)
- **Other:**
  - There is no low-level PvP protection on PvP realms (Aggrend, podcast ep. 4). `official` — n:20261017-no-lowbie-pvp-protection
  - The world-refresh prompt can no longer be accepted in combat — n:20261013-world-refresh-layer-switch (updated)
- **New tools:**
  - **Polestar** (fitzforge/polestar, ~100 stars in 4 days) is an open-source guide addon with simulated 1–60 routes. Its model puts 1–60 at 117–121 /played h, which is an estimate, not a measurement. `reported` — n:20261017-polestar-guide-addon
  - **Everloot** publishes crowdsourced drop, vendor and trainer facts once 3+ installs report them. `reported` — n:20261017-everloot-open-data
- Context, no note:
  - "Addressing the Arguments for Low Dungeon XP" (154 posts) has only one measured report: Excavation Site with 4 players, about ⅔ of a level (27→28.7) in ~2 h with rested XP, at ~120 XP per elite.
  - The scarce gathering materials stop dropping above level 25 (#375).
  - Over 1 million characters were created in the beta.
