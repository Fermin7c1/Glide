# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-22

The stress test ran yesterday. The beta came back around 01:49 UTC today with a forced client update, build 1.60.1.70532. Daybreak's table diff shows no game-data changes against 70450, and Blizzard posted no patch notes. The beta closes for good on Friday, Oct 23 at 10:00 PDT. Today's main new information is Blizzard's official camping article from Oct 21.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Camping is now official, with numbers.**
  - A Basic Campfire (Cooking 1) holds up to 3 more features, one per contributing player. Upgraded campfires hold 5 or 10.
  - All features share a 1-hour cooldown.
  - Sitting or crafting at the camp for 1 minute gives you the other features' benefits.
  - Each profession's first feature unlocks at skill 20. Features needing skill 140+ drop in dungeons and raids.
  - The Leatherworking Camp Tent gives rested XP up to 5% of a level, and does nothing if you already have more. This makes the tent note official.
  - Features: Fishing Bowl +8% stats, Skinning Camp Chair +2% crit, Blacksmithing Sharpening Wheel +6-34 Str, plus Stamina, Intellect, Spirit and mp5 items and the Enchanting armor item.
  - Each camp buff is exclusive with the class buff it copies.

  `official`, model-impact — n:20261022-camp-features-official, n:20261010-camp-tent-rested-5pct (updated)
- **Legacy perks that cut downtime:**
  - Thrill of Adventure restores 1-5% of max HP and mana over 10 s per killing blow on a non-trivial mob. It doesn't work in instances.
  - The Quick and the Dead gives +5/10% ghost speed and free helpful spells for 1-2 min after you're resurrected.
  - Field Medicine cuts Recently Bandaged by 5/10 s.
  - Beta testers say the kill-heal nearly removes eating for melee ("Legacy Talents make Warrior leveling trivial", 65 replies).

  `corroborated` (Mobalytics, Warcraft Tavern, forum) — n:20261022-legacy-downtime-perks
- **Four starter quests pay different XP in game than in the files, according to ForeverDB's "seen in game" list:**

  - Shhh! We're Hunting Kobolds (91772): 270 → 360
  - Legging It (96821): 410 → 550
  - Bloodtalon Matriarch (97223): 700 → 775
