# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-27

Name reservation opens today, and launch is Nov 4 at 23:00 UTC. On the evening of Oct 26 (~20:35 UTC), after yesterday's run, Blizzard posted one more dev notes update: a final racial pass. It changes racials only. There's no new client build after 70532, and the bug tracker has been quiet since Oct 26 08:42 UTC, with no new designer comments.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Tauren Plainsrunning now reaches 60% run speed on foot.** It gives 2% per stack (was 1%) up to 60% (was 30%), and the mounted +3% is gone. At the old build-up rate of 1 stack per 5 s, full speed takes about 150 s of running. Standing still or getting hit drops stacks, and it doesn't combine with Ghost Wolf or Cheetah. Before a mount, a Tauren on a long run gets close to mount speed. Check the build-up rate on day 1.

  `official` — n:20261027-plainsrunning-60pct-on-foot
- **The rest of the racial final pass:**
  - Human Spirit +10% (was 5%), and Ruthless crit is down to 1%.
  - Dwarf: Earth Fury is replaced by +1% hit.
  - Orc: Savage is removed, and Command is back (+5% Hunter/Warlock pet damage).
  - Tauren Endurance is now just +5% HP.
  - Skyborne haste is down to 2%.
  - Night Elf, Gnome, Undead and Troll are unchanged.

  `official` — n:20261027-racials-oct26-final-pass
- **Skyborne Skysight and Read Leyline reworked.**
  - Skysight gives 15% speed for 15 s. Casting it at an elemental convergence gives 1 h of "Charged", during which Skysight gives 30%.
  - Read Leyline gives 50% regen for 15 s. At a Ley Line it gives 1 h of "Attuned" (100% regen).
  - Both buffs persist through death.

  `official` — n:20261027-skyborne-skysight-leyline-rework
- **Downtime racials in numbers.**
  - Troll Rapid Regeneration: 50% HP over 6 s, 3 min cooldown.
  - Undead Cannibalize: 35% HP and mana over 10 s, 2 min cooldown, on humanoid or undead corpses only.

  These are players reading the tooltips; nothing has been measured.

  `reported` — n:20261027-troll-undead-sustain-numbers

  - Forever GuideMate: free GPL, with A/H 1-60 casual routes, class quests and dungeon chains.

    `reported` — n:20261027-guidemate-addon
  - QuestPact: shares quest progress, and you can follow a partner's turn-in route.

    `reported` — n:20261027-questpact-addon
