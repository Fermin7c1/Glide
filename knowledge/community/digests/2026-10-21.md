# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-21

There's still no new client build: 1.60.1.70450 is the latest on forever-ref, The Forever Era and Daybreak. The beta went offline today for the stress test (2-6 p.m. PDT). Blizzard extended it, so it reopens afterwards at cap 30 and closes for good on Friday Oct 23 at 10:00 PDT, possibly after one more build (Wowhead). Today's news comes from Blizzard's Oct 20 dungeon, raid and name-reservation posts, from a batch of designer answers on forever-bugs, and from an unannounced Mage hotfix.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Blizzard gave official level ranges for the nine new dungeons.**
  - Hall of Thanes is 13-18 and Ruins of Lordaeron 15-20.
  - Excavation Site is 24-29 and City of Dalaran 28-33. Dalaran needs an attunement chain: Alliance start with An Alarming Request in Southshore, Horde with Key to the City in Tarren Mill.
  - Drowned City is 35-40 (underwater west of Grom'gol) and Krol'dok Stronghold 40-45 (Riverglades).
  - Alcaz Prison is 48-53, Blackmaw Hold 55-60 (Azshara) and Shaper's Terrace 58-60 (Un'Goro).
  - These are tighter than the fan ranges the knowledge base uses (HoT 13-20, RoL 15-22, Excavation 26-33).

  `official`, model-impact — n:20261021-new-dungeon-levels-official (and n:20261011-dungeon-level-ranges updated)
- **The quest log holds 40 quests, not Classic's 20.** MrGM found it in the client on Sept 16, testers confirmed it, and Bolverk Games and Digital Citizen reported it. Glide's quest-log heuristics still assume 20.

  `corroborated`, model-impact — n:20261021-quest-log-40
- **Highland Hides (98815, Excavation Site) pays 1,150 XP.** The dungeon's other quests pay about 6,100 (forever-bugs #416), and Warcraft Tavern also calls the quest bugged.

  `corroborated`, model-impact — n:20261021-highland-hides-low-xp
- **Some group quests at dungeon entrances pay normal quest XP, without the dungeon bonus (#417).**
  - Collecting Memories (167) and Oh Brother... (168) for the Deadmines.
  - Trouble at the Docks (959) and The Essence of Aku'mai (6563).
  - Shadowshard Fragments (7070) and Overmaster Pyron (4262), which the reporter couldn't check in game.

  `reported`, model-impact — n:20261021-dungeon-quests-missing-bonus-xp
- **Spell haste lowering the GCD is intended.** The floor is 1.0 s and the ceiling 1.5 s. Aidan Moon closed #389 as Not a Bug.

  `official`, model-impact — n:20261018-spell-haste-lowers-gcd (updated)
- **Mage hotfix on Oct 20, with no patch notes.**
  - Elemental Precision is now 3 ranks of 2% hit.
  - Piercing Ice is removed, so Frost loses 6% damage.
  - Ice Barrier r1 absorbs 493. It also gives +3% Frost damage for 1 min, plus 3% while the shield holds.
  - Frost Ward r1 absorbs 178, plus 10% of Frost spell power. Talents were reset.
  - Sources: Reddit via ForeverDB, plus ForeverChanges. The beta was down for hotfixes around 20:00 UTC.

  `corroborated` — n:20261021-mage-frost-talent-hotfix-oct20
- **Pet hit is fixed (#394).**
  - Hunter pets get melee hit equal to your ranged hit, and spell hit of 17/8 × that.
  - Warlock pets get spell hit equal to your spell hit, and melee hit of 8/17 × that.

  `official` — n:20261021-pet-hit-inheritance
- **Racial base stats go back to Classic offsets (#414, fixed).** The two Skyborne races get their own, different offsets.

  `official` — n:20261020-racial-base-stats-modern (updated)
- **Racial fixes:**
  - Touch of the Grave no longer misses almost every time (#369).
  - Earth Fury no longer needs a mace, but its +2% crit damage doesn't work yet (#392).
  - Tauren Endurance's +2% crit damage is a known issue (#396).

  `official` — n:20261021-racial-fixes-oct21
- **Riverglades covers "much of the mid-30s to mid-40s".**
  - It has 150+ new quests, according to Blizzard's video via ForeverDB, and Mount Hyjal is the end of leveling.
  - ForeverDB also found a "Krol'dok Speed" spell in the client. It raises run speed in Stranglethorn and Riverglades, but the amount is unknown.

  `official` / `reported` — n:20261020-riverglades-hyjal-30-60 (updated), n:20261021-kroldok-speed-buff

  `reported` — n:20261021-auto-shot-melee-reset

  - You can reserve up to 3 names across rulesets, but only with a pre-purchased upgrade.
  - The beta closes Oct 23 at 10:00 PDT, and raids unlock Dec 9.

  `official` — n:20261010-name-reservation-oct27, n:20261009-beta-launch-dates (updated)

## New sources (candidates unless noted)
- **MittenQuest (rneuwi):**
  - A 1-60 guide addon for both factions, with routes simulated on time and drop-rate kill counts.
  - Players can submit pace logs. It has quest pins for 3,200+ quests.
- **NorgannonDB (zmarotrix):**
  - An open, crowd-sourced Forever database covering quests, NPCs, objects, items and world data.
  - It's built from QuestieDB and pfQuest plus the client tables, and a Collector addon fills it in.
  - The same author makes Clairvoyance and Oracle.
- **Surveyor (Geraldlol):** exact discovery-XP area borders traced from the client terrain data.
- **petllama Polestar packs:** MittenQuest, Lodestar and RXP free guides, converted to Polestar format.
- **Other addons and tools:**
  - SpeedrunSplits (/played per level)
  - GuideAddon (Hungarian, 1-30)
  - ForeverTracker (client vs server diff tool)
- **Sites:** Blizzard Watch is active, and Bolverk Games is a candidate.
- **Status change:** ForeverDB is promoted to active, since its news/articles check claims against the client.
