# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-09

New beta build Oct 8 (1.60.1.70291). Next build expected Oct 15. Beta stays at cap 30 until it ends Oct 22; launch Nov 4.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Party XP now uses your own level** (Era rule). It used to follow the highest-level member, so lowbies in mixed groups got very little. If any member is far above the mob, nobody gets XP (intended). `official`, model-impact — n:20261009-party-xp-own-level
- **Dungeon mob kill XP up ~20%.** This partly reverses the earlier cut. Aggrend says optimal dungeon leveling should now be about as fast as optimal questing, but not at Era rates, and dungeon cleaving to 60 won't come back. `official`, model-impact — n:20261009-dungeon-kill-xp-up-20, n:20261009-dungeon-vs-quest-parity
- **Rage rework for questing Warriors and Druids.** Rage is now tuned around 20–40% armor instead of tank armor, and three Rage bugs are fixed: it used your health, ignored crits and counted absorbs. Druid crit Rage is +100%. `official` — n:20261009-rage-rebalance-questing
- **Tirisfal Glades:** quest and mob levels raised around Shadowvale and Bandarion Keep. New numbers not published yet. `official`, model-impact
- **Desolace:** the new Gelkis/Magram chain ("Valley of Bones") now needs level 35 (was 30). `official`
- **Hillsbrad "Elixir of Pain":** Stanley's kill XP moved into the quest reward. `official`, model-impact
- **Respawns and drops:** faster for Durotar eggs, Redridge Blackrock Blockade, Teldrassil Sethir and the Shaman totem quest. Gloomrise in Mulgore respawns more evenly. Fewer ambushers in Loch Modan. `official`, model-impact
- **Low ranks of Wrath, Frostbolt, Fireball, Arcane Missiles, Smite, Lightning Bolt and Shadow Bolt** retuned so each new rank is an upgrade. Ret Aura and Thorns went back to Vanilla damage. `official`
- **Hunter:** Improved Concussive Shot's 100% proc was a bug and is now "correct" (value unknown). Yesterday's note is now `contradicted`. A forum test shows fast pets do ~26% more DPS (1.2 vs 2.0 speed), and Claw adds ~20% (`reported`). Auto Shot still has a 0.1–0.2 s cast and ranged code is being reworked (`official`).
- **Shaman:** Shield Specialization and Weapon Mastery are not baseline. The article was wrong (Zirene). `official`
- **Legacy perks that speed leveling:** Talented (talents up to 5 levels earlier), Well Rested, Field Guide (shorter camp cooldown), Frequent Flier (−50% cost, +20% flight speed per the client tree), Permanence and Gourmand. You can spend 16 points per character at launch. Your first character earns its first point at 25 or at 150 crafting. Beta characters got 16 points on Oct 8. `official` / numbers `reported`, model-impact
- **XP buffs to stack:** crafted food gives +5% kill XP for 15 min, and the Cozy Sleeping Bag gives +1% per minute napped, up to +3% for 1 h, with a 1 h cooldown. The bag's quest chain starts at 14 (Westfall 37,50 / Barrens 46,74 → Stonetalon → Loch Modan 49,12 → Hillsbrad). Both stack with camp rested. `corroborated`, model-impact
- **Beta warning:** characters logged out in Loch Modan or Badlands can't log in since Oct 8. `corroborated`
