# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-20

There's still no new build (forever-ref is at 1.60.1.70450), no new dev-notes post (the last was Oct 16 00:15 UTC), and no Forever blue post since the Oct 16 stress-test notice. The stress test is tomorrow (Oct 21, 2 p.m. PDT / 19:00 CEST). The news today comes from designer answers on forever-bugs and from an Aggrend reply on X.

## Leveling-relevant
- **The Applejack Still XP exploit is fixed for launch.**
  - On the beta, the Applejack Still turn-in in Elwynn (quest 91736, Thunder Falls) could be repeated. Its XP scales with level: about 170 XP per turn-in, and a stream showed 250-350k XP/hour.
  - Aggrend replied on X on Oct 19: "Not going to be a thing. Already fixed for launch."
  - Don't route repeat turn-ins.

  `official`, model-impact — n:20261018-applejack-still-repeatable-xp (updated)
- **Holy Diver (87760, Darkshore) should be fixed for launch** (Aidan Moon, forever-bugs #399, closed as Fixed). It gives 0 XP only on the beta, so keep it in launch routes.

  `official`, model-impact — n:20261019-holy-diver-zero-xp (updated)
- **Mage: Arcane Missiles never procs Missile Barrage.**
  - The designer says that was removed in development as too strong, even though the Oct 15 notes list it (#405).
  - A proc consumed with less than 1 s left currently gives an unbuffed 3-missile channel. That's fixed for launch (#397).

  `official` — n:20261020-missile-barrage-no-am-proc
- **Thrown weapons: the first throw will wind up in 0.5 s at launch.** It currently takes the weapon's speed, e.g. 2.0 s (#408, "Fix for launch"). This matters for Warrior and Rogue pulls.

  `official`, model-impact — n:20261020-thrown-windup-fix-launch
- **Leveling past 30 is in Riverglades and Mount Hyjal.**
  - Blizzard's video "Your Leveling Journey Past 30" came out on Oct 19.
  - Icy Veins says Alliance reach Riverglades by a new path from Redridge. The Alliance hub is Farhold Keep, and the Horde work with the Boulderrock ogres.
  - Riverglades also has Krol'dok Stronghold, an outdoor Twilight's Hammer area. Hyjal is the end of the leveling journey, and the Barrow Deeps raid is there.
  - No level ranges were given.

  `official` — n:20261020-riverglades-hyjal-30-60
- **The Skyborne Dalaran→Stormwind portal is still missing.** Both forever-bugs #380 and a forum thread report it, and Blizzard hasn't replied.

  `corroborated`, model-impact — n:20261014-skyborne-only-dalaran-portals (updated)
- **Layering (Wowhead, Oct 17):**
  - Leaving a group moves both the leaver and the leader to new layers.
  - Crossing some zone borders swaps your layer: Dalaran–Silverpine and Tirisfal–Ruins of Lordaeron.
  - A warning now shows before a swap. Tim Jones calls the system a work in progress.

  `reported` — n:20261020-layer-swap-group-zone-border
- **Group quest drops (bug reports #412 and #413):**
  - Frosthowl (98326, Dun Morogh) drops one Sack of Fish per group kill.
  - An Old History Book (2794, Duskwood) drops for one member only.

  `reported` — n:20261020-group-quest-items-single-drop
- **Rage from incoming damage (#409):**
  - Spell damage gives rage based on the spell's level.
  - Mana-using NPCs give 40-45% less rage than expected.
  - Defensive Stance takes 10% off rage from incoming damage.
  - The designer has asked for test cases.

  `reported` — n:20261020-rage-incoming-damage-anomalies
- **Racial base stats use modern offsets (#414).** For example, Orc is +3/-3/+1/-1/0 against Human, where Classic is +3/-3/+2/-3/+3. Night Elf is -2/+2/0/0/0, where Classic is -3/+5/-1/0/0.

  `reported` — n:20261020-racial-base-stats-modern

  - **Zockify's 70450 changelog** says Resurrection Sickness goes "1 → 10 min". It also lists Champion of the Light 10/20/30 → 20/40/60, which is the reverse of foreverchanges' 70425 entry. The diff looks inverted, and forever-bugs #25 still reports 1 min after 70425. n:20261017-res-sickness-1min-after-70425 is unchanged.
  - **Small city changes (Reddit via Icy Veins):** Darnassus has a new mailbox by the AH. In Stormwind, an invisible wall between the barber shop and the AH was removed.
  - **Forum, no staff reply:**
    - "Leveling too fast": one claim puts 1-20 at 20.5 h versus Era's 20.4 h.

  - forever-bugs: Revelation enchant procs have a downranking penalty (#401). Throwing arrows' textures are fixed. Dark Sacrifice CC is fixed for live.
