# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-14

Another quiet day for official posts. The dev notes and known issues are still the Oct 8 versions. The only Forever blue post was Kaivax's anti-RMT "Temporary Economy Holds" (Oct 13), which doesn't affect leveling. A small client build, 70374, went out on Oct 13 and changed no game data; the designer-confirmed Oct 15 build is still pending. The useful finds came from the data side. Factoruide is a new route optimizer whose data pins down the new boat routes and Riverglades access.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Oct 15 build fix list grows to 16 designer-confirmed fixes.** New since yesterday:
  - Restorative Totems now boosts Healing Stream Totem (#291).
  - Summon Hawk no longer chases your target (#300).
  - Mind Vision no longer interrupts drinking (#321).
  - Talent tooltips show ranks again (#217).
  - Legacy Bountiful Harvest and Luremaster now give their extra materials and fish (#309, #310).
  - Traps scale with AP/SP, and a frost and a fire trap can be up together (#283).

  `official` — n:20261013-oct15-build-confirmed-fixes (sources: forever-bugs #303, foreverchanges.pro)
- **Client build 1.60.1.70374 (Oct 13).** It changes no items, spells, talents or recipes compared with 70338. The forever-ref diff is empty, and wowclassicforever.info's file diff shows 666 files changed, all of them sound, map textures and interface manifests. `corroborated` — n:20261014-client-build-70374
- **The Menethil boat runs a one-way loop.** The route is Menethil → Southshore (~6 min) → Auberdine (~3.5 min) → Menethil (~3 min, no stop), and Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine (~2.5 min) runs both ways. That means:
  - Southshore → Menethil goes on through Auberdine (~6.5 min).
  - Menethil → Auberdine goes through Southshore (~9.5 min).

  Ride times are estimates from client path length (Factoruide), not timed in game. Updated `corroborated` — n:20261010-ship-menethil-southshore-auberdine
- **Riverglades access: a neutral boat, Steamwheedle Port (Tanaris) ↔ Powderfuse Port, ~3 min.** The zone is about levels 35–45. `corroborated` (classicwowforever.com + client path) — n:20261014-riverglades-boat-tanaris
- **The Dalaran ↔ Stormwind Wizard's Sanctum portals are reported to be Skyborne-only.** Other Alliance races walk to Dalaran (City of Dalaran dungeon, 28–33). `reported` (Factoruide changelog, citing players) — n:20261014-skyborne-only-dalaran-portals
- **No shared mob tagging.** Blizzard must "hold the line" (Greenfield, Sept 21) and will fix problem quests instead. Group up for contested kill quests. This was missing from the KB. `official` — n:20261014-no-shared-mob-tagging
- **Open stress test Oct 19.** It's a launcher entry for accounts without beta access, running for several hours; the beta ends Oct 22. Expect crowded layers. `corroborated` — n:20261014-stress-test-oct19
- **Factoruide** is an app plus addon that simulates 1–60 and searches for the fastest personal route. It models race, class, professions, dungeons, group, rested, hearth and teleports, and is built on a merged Forever SQLite DB. `reported` — n:20261014-factoruide-route-optimizer
- Context, no new note: an Inven Global interview (Oct 8, Clay Stone) says dungeon XP tuning will continue through the beta, aiming for a "middle ground" by launch. Camping past level 30 is being watched. A lone forum report says Survival's Resourcefulness never procs (0 in about 50 crits); not noted until it's confirmed.
