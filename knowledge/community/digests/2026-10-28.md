# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-28

Name reservation is on day 2, and launch is Nov 4 at 23:00 UTC. Two things are new since yesterday:

- **A racial addendum.** Kaivax posted a second development-notes post on Oct 27 (#8).
- **New client builds.** Three builds came after 70532: 70630, 70634 and 70658.
  - Build 70630 changed 80 data rows. 70634 and 70658 changed nothing that the trackers watch.
  - The 70658 client data now includes the Oct 20-23 server hotfixes, plus a few unannounced Hunter and Shaman number changes.

On the bug tracker, no designer has replied since Oct 26.

  - Troll Berserking lasts 15 s (was 10).
  - Undead Touch of the Grave internal cooldown is 15 s (was 25).
