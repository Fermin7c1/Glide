Name reservation day 3; launch is Nov 4 at 23:00 UTC. The news today is a new build, 1.60.1.70685, and two unannounced server hotfixes found in the data: a Mage talent reshuffle that also seems to remove Frost Blast, and the Hunter Aspect of the Hawk / Trueshot Aura cut, now confirmed by several trackers. ## Leveling-relevant
- **Mage hotfix on 70685 (talents).**
  - Improved Fireball is now **Accelerant**: 5 ranks, -0.1 to -0.5 s cast on Fireball, Frostfire Bolt and Arcane Wrath.
  - Elemental Precision is now **Precision**: 3 ranks, +2/4/6% hit with all spells.
  - **Critical Mass** 3 ranks (2/4/6% Fire crit, was 5 ranks). **Incineration** 5 ranks (2-10%, was 3).
  - **Arcane Focus** removed. **Ice Lance rank 1** gets an 8 s cooldown (rank 6 has none).
  - Old Mage talent builds no longer fit the tree: re-plan leveling talents.

  `corroborated` — n:20261029-mage-talent-hotfix-70685 (wowsims PR #744, forever-info PR #10)
- **Frost Blast removed?** The same hotfix data drops all three Frost Blast ranks (spells 1326053-55, learned at 24/36/48). Wowhead tooltips still mention it in four talents. Check the trainer at 24 before planning Frost AoE around it.

  `reported` — n:20261029-frost-blast-removed-hotfix (wowsims PR #744)
- **Client build 70685.** Only 3 client rows changed: a new "Camping" spell, a new Adventurer's Tent description, and a spell category (shared cooldown group) for the tent. The new text and values aren't public yet; read the tent tooltip on day 1.

  `corroborated` — n:20261029-client-build-70685 (daybreakforever, forever-info, wowsims)

  - Hunter: Aspect of the Hawk 15/22/30/38/47/55 and Trueshot Aura 15/22/30/40 are a server hotfix live on the launch realms since Oct 28 (ForeverChanges, ForeverDB, WCLBox, forever-info, US forum). Now `corroborated` (n:20261028-hunter-hawk-trueshot-70658).
