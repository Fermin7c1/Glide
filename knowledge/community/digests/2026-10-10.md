# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-10

No new Blizzard posts Oct 9–10. Two small client builds landed (70334, 70338) with almost no data changes; the leveling build is still 70291 (Oct 8). Zirene keeps marking fixes "Fixed October 15th". Beta ends Oct 22 at cap 30; launch Nov 4.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Downranking nerf.** Ranks far below your level get less spell power and fewer talent procs. Blizzard's example: Rank 1 Frostbolt at 60 has a 0% Frostbite chance. `official` — n:20261010-downranking-penalty
- **Downrank formula (measured).** A rank keeps its full coefficient for 17 levels after you learn it, then loses 5% per level, reaching 0 at +37. Measured on heals up to 30. `reported` — n:20261010-downrank-formula-5pct
- **Caster rank smoothing, client numbers.** Frostbolt R1 per-level gain dropped 0.5 → 0.2 and R4 base rose 60 → 70. Thorns 22 → 18, Ret Aura 30 → 20. Low ranks scale less, so train every new rank. `official` (updated) — n:20261009-caster-rank-smoothing
- **Priest Penance cut.** Damage and healing are lower, rank 1 costs 50% more mana, and R4 (92) now beats R3 (72). The forum backlash is large. `official` — n:20261010-penance-nerf
- **Hunter traps scale from Oct 15.** Immolation and Explosive Trap ignore AP/SP today. From Oct 15 they scale, and one Frost plus one Fire trap can be active together (Aidan Moon). `official` — n:20261010-hunter-traps-scale-oct15
- **Mage AoE farming nerfed.** Improved Blizzard's slow is capped at 40% (was 65%), Cone of Cold is shorter, Arcane Concentration procs less from low ranks, and Frostfire Bolt comes at 40. `official` — n:20261010-mage-aoe-farm-nerfs
- **Shaman QoL.** Imbues last 60 min (was 5) and stack with stones and oils. Totem GCD is 1 s and totems last 5 min. Totemic Recall and Call of the Elements come at 20. Lightning Bolt R4+ is a 2.5 s cast. `official` — n:20261010-shaman-imbue-totem-qol
- **Warlock.** Pets scale with your stats, Life Tap scales with Spirit, Drain Soul's shard chance rises each tick, and Firestone/Spellstone are now imbues. `official` — n:20261010-warlock-leveling-changes
- **Rogue.** Sap no longer breaks Stealth, poisons stack with sharpening stones, and Feint threat is doubled. `official` — n:20261010-rogue-leveling-changes
- **Student Fodder gives no rested XP** (in SoD it gave ~20% of a level). It now grants Trail Snack: +60% speed and a heal over time. `reported` (one blog) — n:20261010-student-fodder-no-rested
- **Camp Tent (client data):** tops rested up to 5% of a level, once per hour, after 30 s sitting near a campfire. `reported`, model-impact — n:20261010-camp-tent-rested-5pct
- **Well Rested perk (client data):** rested builds 20% faster and the cap is +20%, matching the guide's +4% × 5 ranks. `reported` (updated), model-impact — n:20261009-legacy-perk-numbers
- **Datamined undead XP buff:** Archmagister's Wisdom/Perception give +10% XP from Undead kills for 5 min. Source item unknown; possibly Archmagister's Faceted Pendant, which Wowhead's data added Oct 10. `reported`, model-impact — n:20261010-archmagister-undead-xp
- **New ships:** Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine, plus Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine. No sailing times yet. `corroborated`, model-impact — n:20261010-ship-menethil-southshore-auberdine
- **Racials:** Aggrend says Blood Fury and Berserking may be nerfed, and the weapon-skill racials may change in next week's build. `official` — n:20261010-racial-nerfs-next-week
- **Loch Modan/Badlands login hang:** a server hiccup, fixed Oct 9. `official` (updated) — n:20261009-loch-modan-login-bug
- Small client builds 70334/70338: n:20261010-client-builds-70334-70338. Name reservation reportedly opens Oct 27 (`reported`): n:20261010-name-reservation-oct27.
