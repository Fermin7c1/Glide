# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-11

A quiet weekend. Blizzard made no posts on Oct 10–11 in any region. The dev notes and known issues are still the Oct 8 versions, and the leveling build is still 70291. The next build is expected Oct 15 (Zirene closed the Immolation Trap scaling bug as "Fixed" today). Beta ends Oct 22 at cap 30. Name reservation runs Oct 27 – Nov 3, and launch is Nov 4.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Resurrection Sickness at low level.** In the beta, a spirit-healer res gives the full 10 minutes from level 10 (Era: 1 min at 10, +1 per level). Blizzard's known issues list "duration is not correct at lower levels", still open. At 10–19, corpse-run. `corroborated` — n:20261011-res-sickness-10min-from-10
- **Spirit healer durability loss is 25%** (was 50%) since the Oct 8 build. `reported` — n:20261011-spirit-healer-durability-25
- **Hunter: Truthseeker's Bow now requires level 40** (was 30). Hotfixed retroactively around Oct 9–10. Don't plan it as a level-30 upgrade. `reported` — n:20261011-truthseeker-bow-level-40
- **Shaman L30 chain "The Tempest's Weapons":**
  - Steps: Charlga (RFK), then 20 twisted totems (Dustwallow 36–39, Desolace 37–39, Swamp of Sorrows 34–37, Badlands 39+), then the level-40 elite event in Thousand Needles. Turn in at Bath'rah, Alterac.
  - The Oct 8 build sped up totem-mob respawns, raised totem drops, and made Eye of the Tempest multi-drop.
  - `corroborated` — n:20261011-shaman-tempest-weapons-chain
- **Hunter pet snapshot bug.** With any point in Focused Fire, pet stats only update on re-summon, so dismiss and call the pet after upgrades. `reported` — n:20261011-hunter-pet-focused-fire-snapshot
- **Improved Mend Pet bug.** At 2/2 it raises Mend Pet's cost (155 → 186). Skip it for now. `reported` — n:20261011-improved-mend-pet-cost-bug
- **Cozy Sleeping Bag duration conflict.** Guides say the +3% lasts 1 h. Forever's loading-screen tip says "3 minutes … 3% more experience for two hours". If it's 2 h on a 1 h cooldown, the buff can stay up permanently. `corroborated` (updated), model-impact — n:20261009-cozy-sleeping-bag
- **Beta /played to ~30:** 21–64 h across 9 characters, median ~34 h; Warlocks were fastest (21–22 h). Calibration only. `reported` — n:20261011-beta-played-to-30
- **Dungeon level ranges** for the 14 dungeons, from foreverdb.net. Examples: RFC 13–18, HoT 13–20, RoL 15–22, Excavation Site: Wetlands 26–33, Uldaman 41–51. A community Alliance dungeon plan is attached. `reported`, model-impact — n:20261011-dungeon-level-ranges
- **RestedXP:** Forever questing routes 1–35 are done for both factions; 1–20 is free. A dungeon guide is next, and 35–60 comes after launch. `corroborated` — n:20261011-restedxp-forever-1-35
