# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-26

The beta stays closed. Character creation and name reservation open tomorrow (Tue Oct 27), and launch is Nov 4 at 23:00 UTC. There were no new Blizzard dev notes, news posts or blue posts on Oct 24-26. The only designer activity was Aidan Moon closing bug-tracker issues. There was no run on Oct 25, so this digest covers Oct 24 15:30 UTC onward.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Spell haste does not shorten channels.** Aidan Moon closed #437 as Not A Bug: Mind Flay, Drain Life, Evocation and Wrack keep their full channel time at any haste, like DoTs. Haste still shortens casts and the GCD.

  `official` — n:20261026-channeled-spells-not-hasted
- **Warlock Agony ticks now follow your current spell power.** Aidan Moon (#398, Fixed): every tick updates to your spell power, while Amplify Curse is snapshotted at cast.

  `official` — n:20261026-agony-dynamic-spellpower
- **Shadowmeld can't open from stealth.** Aidan Moon (#366, Not A Bug): no Ambush, Cheap Shot, Pounce or Ravage from Shadowmeld.

  `official` — n:20261026-shadowmeld-no-stealth-openers
- **Layering fixes for launch.** Clay Stone (associate production director) told GameSpot on Oct 23 that groups will no longer be split after boat or zeppelin loading screens, or when large groups enter or leave capitals. Joining a group still moves you to the leader's layer, and zone-border swaps and World Refresh stay.

  `official` — n:20261026-layering-transport-capital-fix
- **The live client is `_forever_`, on build 70532.** Pre-install works now. It installs to `World of Warcraft\_forever_`, and the live product serves 1.60.1.70532, the same build the beta ended on. The project's test PC already has `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\Glide`.

  `corroborated` — n:20261026-live-client-forever-folder
- **Procs reported dead on the final client.** Deep Wounds never procs (#434, video), and players also report Windfury Weapon and the Revelation enchant failing. All three worked earlier in beta. There's no Blizzard answer, and live uses the same build, so check on day 1.

  `reported` — n:20261026-proc-effects-broken-beta-end
- **ForeverDB will publish crowdsourced XP/hour.** Addon 0.48-0.49 (Oct 25) times active play per level. From Nov 4, foreverdb.net/leveling-route shows the median XP/hour per zone and level. There's no download, so it's for post-launch checks of the route model.

  `reported` — n:20261026-foreverdb-crowd-xph
- **Group cave farming is slower than questing.** A 5-man at level 15 in Darkshore and Westfall caves earns about 25k XP/hour, which the poster puts at about half a strong quester's pace. Single forum claim.

  `reported` — n:20261026-hyperspawn-cave-group-xph
- **Updated hotfix notes:**
  - Righteous Fury at 10 is now confirmed by a second tracker, wowclassicforever.info (n:20261024-righteous-fury-level-10, `corroborated`).
  - Champion of the Light at 20/40/60% is in the 70532 data per three hotfix readers, but it still conflicts with Blizzard's Oct 15 notes, so check the tooltip on launch (n:20261024-hotfix-champion-of-the-light, `corroborated`).
  - The sources disagree on Holy Fire rank 1: foreverdb says 51-65 → 48-60, and foreverchanges says 57-70 → 53-65.
- **Context, no note:**
  - **RestedXP:** The forum thread "So, the RestedXP add-on" (142 posts) has no staff reply. One player says the addon is free to 30 and the guide covers 30-60. On the EU forums, players question the Journey guide (getjourney.gg) under the add-on policy, with an MVP reply only.
  - **Unsourced dungeon XP/hour claim:** Spell-cleave groups make 80-100k XP/hour in SM and ZF.
  - **Racials:** Players report the Human weapon racial is gone in favor of a flat 2% crit. This matches the racial rework note (n:20261017-racials-oct15). Touch of the Grave has a 25 s cooldown, per gamer.org.
  - **Not leveling:**
