# WoW Forever community digest — 2026-10-18

It was a quiet weekend. There's no new build (still 1.60.1.70450) and no new development-notes post. Kaivax's only Forever blue posts since Oct 16 are the stress-test notice and "Datamined in the Latest Beta Build" (the datamined art assets aren't meant for Forever). The useful finds are a possibly repeatable XP quest in Elwynn, a Westfall food that speeds you up, and clearer layering rules.

## Leveling-relevant
- **Elwynn's Applejack Still may give repeatable XP.**
  - Quest 91736 is an object north of Westbrook Garrison, at about 24.6, 58.0.
  - It takes 4 Shiny Red Apples, which Homer Stonefield sells at the Stonefield farm. It gives a Thunder Applejack and 100 XP.
  - A beta player says the turn-in repeats without limit and asked whether that's an exploit. No staff reply yet.
  - You need one turn-in anyway, for "An Apple Treat" (630 XP).

  `reported` — n:20261018-applejack-still-repeatable-xp
- **Westfall Stew gives +15% run speed while in Westfall, plus the +5% kill XP of all nutritious food, for 15 min.**
  - It's item 733 and needs level 5. You get it from quest 38 or cook it from the Westfall recipe.
  - Two client-data sources: forever-ref spell 1248688, and metaforge, which says it appeared in build 70170.

  `corroborated`, model-impact — n:20261018-westfall-stew-speed-xp
- **Layering rules:**
  - When someone leaves a group, both the leaver and the leader move to new layers.
  - Neighboring zones can be on different layers, so crossing a border can switch you. Examples: Tirisfal ↔ Ruins of Lordaeron, and Silverpine ↔ Dalaran.
  - Zeppelin rides can also switch you.
  - A warning appears before the move. Tim Jones says it's a work in progress.

  Source: Wowhead Oct 17, Icy Veins Oct 15. `corroborated` — n:20261013-world-refresh-layer-switch (updated)
- **All spell haste lowers the global cooldown on the beta right now** (Berserking, Skyborne 3% haste). The source is a forever-bugs collaborator replying on #389, and the issue is still open. `reported` — n:20261018-spell-haste-lowers-gcd
- **Auto Shot:**
  - The spam trick that skipped the 0.5 s delay is confirmed hotfixed (#361).
  - New open bug: Auto Shot cancels, or switches to melee at range, when you change targets quickly (#386).

  n:20261017-wand-autoshot-stand-still (updated)
- **Undead Touch of the Grave:** the reporter of #369 says the near-100% miss rate "appears to be resolved". EU players still complain about the long gaps between procs since the 25 s ICD. n:20261017-racials-oct15 (updated)
- **Context, no note:**
  - The "Leveling too fast" thread (53 posts) repeats Blizzard's figure of about 20.5 h /played to level 20 in Forever vs 20.4 h in Classic. That's already n:20261008-leveling-speed-same-as-era.
  - The dungeon-XP thread (1,883 posts) had no new measurements.
  - Raid-wide buffs (Oct 15 build) only matter at endgame.
  - Gift of Galloping (+10% mounted speed for 15 days) needs level 60.
  - Elemental Reach no longer extends Flame Shock range (#370, one report).
