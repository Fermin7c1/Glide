# Guides page plan: research notes

Five read-only readers mapped what the Guides page enhancements build on, at commit e423658 (2026-11-02). Line
numbers drift as the code changes: re-check them before editing. The plan that uses these notes is
[guides-explorer.md](guides-explorer.md). Generated from the readers' reports; costs were measured on synthetic data
(the player's kb.db, walking maps and db2 tables exist only on the development PC).

## The Explorer as it is (rxp-browser.ts and friends)

The Explorer already has its own "Guides" page in the left rail. cf08688 put it there and fitted it to the window like the Coach page, and a concurrent session added three more commits while this research ran: 2503c00 (CSS-transform zoom and pan, Follow step, Done steps, Left/Right/Space/+/-/0 keys), 94c41aa (picking only a race or only a class now shows any step one of the allowed characters would see, and Next guide stays in the guide's group) and e423658 (a hover card on pins, pins reachable with Tab, Enter/Space picks one). All line references below are at e423658. Five problems remain. (1) Which steps show, and their numbers, are still worked out by a second copy of RestedXP's rules written in TypeScript. On RestedXP's 80 free guides it disagrees with the knowledge layer the coach uses on 41 guides for a Human Mage, 206 extra steps in all (`step << skip`, SOD/CLASSIC/level words, and season, dungeon, xprate, phase, era/som and conditional tags). (2) Selection, pins, ticks, ticked objectives and sub-waypoints are stored as positions in a filtered list, so they point at the wrong step after a filter change or across zones. (3) The `~N yd` text assumes every zone is 3500 x 2400 yd. (4) Timeline ticks are told apart by colour only and sit under the slider; the step list can't be reached by keyboard. (5) The page is still not "tight": up to three inner scrolling boxes (the current step, the step list, the inspector), a page header that wraps onto several rows, a timeline block about 120 px tall, and at the 900 px minimum window the page stacks and scrolls. Recommended approach. The engine works out which steps show and their numbers with knowledge/rxp_parser and sends the result with each guide as compact per-step bitmasks over every character and the three settings toggles (prototyped: about 55 ms per guide, about 12 distinct masks per guide). The renderer stops evaluating `<<` conditions itself and only reads those masks. All state is keyed by the step's position in the guide (`st.idx`). The map is built once per zone into separate layers, so selecting a step changes classes and the active leg instead of rebuilding everything. Route legs are drawn through RestedXP's own path points (6,504 of the 13,868 gotos in the free guides). The guide window gets a single scroller, and the controls collapse into one toolbar row.

### Gaps and risks

- Which steps show, and their numbers, are still decided by a TypeScript copy of RestedXP's rules. It disagrees with the coach on 41 of 80 free guides, which breaks matching the Explorer's step numbers to the coach and makes 'Jump to live step' impossible until the engine decides them.
- The guide payload is missing data later features need: waypoint cond and action index, unit ids, the item lists of bankdeposit/bankwithdraw/collectmultiple, per-zone yard sizes, any position for kill/loot/use targets, quest prerequisites and chains, step timing, XP and hazards. Most of it lives only in kb.db/glide.db on the player's PC, so the Explorer must say plainly what it can't show without them.
- Rebuilding all the HTML on every step (the list and the whole SVG) loses keyboard focus: a pin picked with Enter loses focus at once. Adding keyboard access to the list and ticks makes this worse until those parts are changed in place instead of rebuilt.
- The timeline ticks sit under the range input (z-index 2 over 1), so their clicks and titles probably never fire. They are told apart by colour only and can't be focused. This is inferred from the CSS and needs checking in the app.
- will-change: transform on the zoom layer probably stops Chromium re-rasterising when the scale changes, which would make the map, pins and text soft at 4-8x. This can't be checked here; check it at 2560x1440 in a muted test copy.
- Layout is still not tight. There are three inner scrolling boxes (current step 42%, step list, inspector 52%). The header with 10 controls wraps to 2+ rows at 1400px. The timeline block is about 120px. At the minimum window (900px wide) the 900px media query stacks the columns and the page scrolls, which goes against the player's 'no side scrolling'.
- Defaults differ: the Explorer starts with SSF off and group quests on, while the coach's DEFAULT_RXP has SSF on and group quests on, and the live page X can override both. Even after the visibility fix, the default numbering won't match the coach unless the Explorer's toggles start from the same settings.
- 'Show all <<' gives dimmed steps their guide step number instead of a RestedXP number, mixing two numberings, and 'of n' counts them.
- Whether the engine state's `step` is RestedXP's live step number (the number under the player's settings) needs checking before 'Jump to live step' maps it through the masks.
- Pins sit at a step's first goto, often one of RestedXP's hidden path points, so pins and legs are misplaced for about 1 in 5 steps. The live coach uses the first goto too, so changing where the pin goes is a choice that affects only the Explorer.
- Another session is committing to these files (3 commits during this research), so the line references will drift and the work needs coordinating.

### Recommended steps

1. Engine visibility from the knowledge layer: in companion/rxp_import.py add a function (e.g. explorer_visibility(text, parsed, base)) called from guide_detail. Characters: the valid (race pick, class) pairs from knowledge/vocab.py (RACE_CLASSES ∩ FACTION_CLASSES, Skyborne once per faction), giving the order in a `players` list such as 'Human/WARRIOR' or 'Skyborne@Horde/MAGE'. Settings: the 8 ssf x group_quests x hardcore combinations, with season/phase/xprate/dungeons from config companion.rxp through RxpSettings.from_options. Evaluate with cond_applies memoised per (cond, player), views.step_flags only when cond_flags exist, views.step_shown per (flags, settings) with views.guide_dungeons(guide text). Send `players`, `settings`, `vis_masks: string[]` (the distinct masks), `step.vis` (index into vis_masks), `step.never`, `action.vis` (a mask over players only, for action conds), `waypoint.cond`, `waypoint.act` (its action's position) and `defaults` (DEFAULT_RXP merged with companion.rxp). Add the types to ipc.ts and a 'contract 4 additive' bullet to REFACTOR-CONTRACTS. Parity test in tests/test_rxp_import.py: for the fixture guides, every (player, settings) numbering read from the masks must equal views.rxp_numbering and analyzer Route.rxp_to_idx.
2. Renderer visibility: remove condApplies/stepFlagsShown and the evaluator in rxp-conditions.ts (keep racePick and the picker test, and rewrite tests/test_rxp_conditions.py around the masks). The picks select a set of characters: a race picks its classes, a class picks the races that can play it, no pick means everyone. A step shows when any bit is set for that set under the current settings combination. Show a RestedXP number only when the set is one character (the running count of set bits); with a partial pick, say how to get exact numbers. Drop never-loaded steps entirely, even with 'Show all <<'. Under 'Show all <<', dim steps whose bits are all 0 and give them no number. Start the toggles from payload.defaults, overridden by the live rxp_settings.
3. Identity: move everything to the guide step number (st.idx). That means activeSrc, a `selected: {kind:'step'|'wp'|'leg'|'loop'|'obj', src, sub?}` replacing pinnedStepIdx, and data-src on pins, list items and ticks, plus a posBySrc map for stepping. On refilter, keep activeSrc, or move to the next visible step after it. Key checkedObjs by `${id}:${index}:${st.idx}:${action.line}`. A sub-waypoint's index is always its position in st.waypoints: the map draws only the current zone's but keeps the original letter and index, the pills use the same letters, and waypoints whose action.vis excludes the picked characters are hidden.
4. Route geometry from RestedXP's own paths: build legs once per model. A stop is any non-'path' waypoint. A step's pin goes on its first stop, not its first goto. leg(prev → cur) = the previous step's last stop, then the current step's path points in order, then its first stop. Draw path points as small dots without letters, and letter only stops. Measure yards along the leg polyline with the zone's w_yd/h_yd, which guide_detail adds to zones[] from kb.db maps or map_bounds via store.map_size_yd; with no size, show no yards rather than guessing. Write leg.points so the walking-route work can later replace them with the engine's navmesh path, without touching the renderer.
5. Restructure the SVG into persistent groups: #rxpb-l-route, #rxpb-l-loops, #rxpb-l-objectives (new, for kill/loot/use targets once the engine supplies them; a shape per kind, a convex outline for spawn clusters), #rxpb-l-pins, #rxpb-l-active and #rxpb-l-hl (quest chain). Rebuild the zone groups only when the model or the map changes; on selectStep only toggle classes and redraw #rxpb-l-active. Restore focus to the pin with the same data-src after any rebuild. Handle every element through one listener on the svg (pointerover, focusin, click, keydown) keyed by data-kind/data-src/data-sub. Give legs an invisible wide hit line. Turn the hover card into a generic element card (step, sub-waypoint, leg with yards, loop, objective target). Make the inspector a compact card anchored to the element that never scrolls; long content belongs in the step list.
6. Zoom/pan polish (the committed transform approach stays): add will-change only while dragging or wheeling (a class on the stage) so the browser re-rasterises at rest. Follow step centres on the active target (targetWp or the step's first stop), not on st.x. Keep the hover card, inspector, HUD and tools outside #rxpb-map-layer, as now. Ignore Ctrl+wheel on the map so Electron's app zoom keeps working.

Move the ticks into their own strip above the slider as <button>s with aria-labels, coded by glyph (! ? ⚔ ✦ ⌂ ☠, the same as the pins) as well as colour; then drop the legend row. Zone tabs get role=tab, aria-selected and arrow keys. In wireKeys add Escape (close the card, clear the highlight) and [ / ] (previous/next sub-waypoint), and let arrow keys act after a toolbar checkbox has been clicked.

## Drawn walking routes

Scope note first. The request the harness relayed asks for the Explorer to get its own icon in the left rail and to stop scrolling twice. docs/REFACTOR-CONTRACTS.md (the last recorded change, "the Explorer is its own page, Guides") and task #5 show that is already done. Drawn routes go beyond that request, so the player should confirm them before anyone builds this.

What exists: the live coach already finds real walking routes. walk_route (knowledge/api/navigation.py:170) runs A* over the walking map in data/gamedata/nav/<continent>.npz, falls back to the older terrain grid in data/gamedata/terrain/<map>.npz, and returns map-percent points, walk/straight/swim yards, run_s, the areas passed through, compass legs and a source. A small context object built outside the coach works with it: SimpleNamespace(kb=store.KB(path), level=n, summary={}). I checked that in this container; with no kb.db it answers {"unavailable": "no map bounds for one end"}.

plan_to_step and walk_to_step need the live game summary, so they can't serve the Explorer. Flights as actually flown come from knowledge/mechanics/taxi.py, which reads the client's taxi tables in data/db2/*.csv. "Dead reckoning" appears nowhere in the code. The nearest things are NavGraph.track (which floor the player is on, from their recent movement), flight_from_track (where a flight is going, from positions flown) and RouteKeeper.view (starts the drawn route at the player). None of them help a guide preview.

All of this data lives only on the player's PC. None of it is in this container, so timings come from a synthetic benchmark and from the seed copy of the free guides:
- A* runs at about 25-33k node expansions a second.
- Loading one continent's walking map takes about 0.2-1 s.
- A big guide has 400-640 legs (median 72 yd, 90th percentile 390-495 yd, 1% over 2 km).
- A leg that can't be reached inside the search window costs up to the 600k-expansion cap, about 20 s.

So a whole guide cannot reliably finish in one call of a few seconds. My recommendation:
- A new engine module, companion/rxp_routes.py, run as `rxp_import.py routes <id> <index>` with the request as JSON on stdin.
- It computes legs for the step order the app shows, nearest the current step first, within a time budget. It caps the search per leg and answers which legs are still pending; the app asks again until none are.
- It keeps each leg's world-space path in an SQLite cache, keyed by continent and the two endpoints and stamped with the walking map's version and file time.
- It draws flights along their real paths, and hearth and deathskip as jumps, never as walks.
- It runs in its own short process, never in the running engine: the walking-map cache keeps only one continent in memory, so an Explorer route on another continent would push out the live coach's map.

### Gaps and risks

- Scope: the relayed request is about where the Explorer sits and the double scroll bar, and REFACTOR-CONTRACTS.md shows the 'Guides' page in the rail was already recorded. Drawn routes go beyond that request; confirm with the player before building.
- No real data here (kb.db, nav or terrain .npz, db2 CSVs), so all costs are estimates. Synthetic A* runs at 25-33k expansions/s, but real expansions per leg on actual terrain, real map load times and the grid fallback's _nav_links build time are unmeasured. Measure on the development PC before fixing budgets.
- Worst case per leg: an endpoint the search window can't reach (an island, a closed building, a wrong floor, an instance entrance) burns up to max_expand=600000 (about 20 s) in _walk_nav, then may try the grid with another 400000. No caller can pass a smaller cap today; navigation.py needs an optional max_expand parameter.
- Without a per-leg cap and a time budget, one CLI call for a 400-640-leg guide can take 10-60 s cold, and pythonJson has no timeout or cancel, so a renderer that fires calls on every scrub piles up Python processes. Allow one request in flight, debounced.
- walk_route converts points only to the start's map and doesn't clamp them, so legs that cross zones need converting to every guide map they touch. The routes command must do that itself from world points.
- The route cache in the live engine's walking-map loader (load_graph) keeps one continent at a time. Running Explorer routes inside the engine process would thrash the coach's map; it must stay in its own process.
- On Windows, CPU-heavy A* in the CLI competes with the game: pythonJson starts it at normal priority. Lower it with stdlib ctypes SetPriorityClass(BELOW_NORMAL) inside the routes command.
- Walking maps take about an hour per continent to build at idle and are missing on fresh installs. The Explorer must show grid or straight legs as approximate (source per leg) and recompute once the map's file time changes (cache stamp).
- Hearth destinations depend on the last .home step, which a filtered step order may hide. Deathskip graveyards and flight origins depend on kb poi. Free or bought guides not yet in kb.db get no step_brief travel data, so the engine must work these out from the parsed guide.
- walk_route's run_s double-counts the 1.25 detour on walked paths (separate task queued). The Explorer should time walked legs with MT.run_time_s directly, or its numbers will disagree with the coach's.
- Walking is not symmetric (one-way drops up to 8 yd, cliffs), so a cached leg A->B must not be reused reversed for B->A.
- Concurrent CLI processes (fast scrubbing, two windows) writing a JSON cache would lose entries. Use stdlib sqlite3 with short transactions; community_feed.py already uses sqlite3, so no new dependency.

The summary carries class, race and faction from page B; guide and guide_group from page G, where the name is RestedXP's zero-padded form cut to 40 bytes; and step, which is core `st` (RXPCData.currentStep, RestedXP's 1-based number among the steps it keeps). It also carries rxp_settings {ssf, group_quests} from page X; hardcore and dungeons are not on the strip. None of these keys is on the light stream the renderer already gets every second (LITE_KEYS), and the code that turns a guide name into a guide only works against kb.db keys (GuideResolver), not the Explorer's packId:index catalog. Step numbering is the bigger trap. The canonical rule is rxp_parser.rxp_numbering, which the coach's Route.rxp_to_idx uses. The Explorer's own TypeScript rule gives different numbers on 14 of the 80 free guides in the seed copy: it ignores dungeon, season, som/phase and conditional step tags, and it reads some condition words ('sod', 'skip') differently. So mapping live step N onto the Explorer's own step number can land up to 18 steps off. Recommended approach: (1) add 8 small keys to the light stream (contract 3 allows added keys; about 200 B/s) and keep them in live.game. (2) Have Python, through a cheap new rxp_import.py command, number the Explorer's own guide text for a player. It reuses player_from, RxpSettings.from_options and rxp_numbering, and works whether or not the coach runs. Also have guide_summary emit the RestedXP page forms of each guide's name and group (affix_name + page_text) so the renderer can match the live guide by equality. (3) In the renderer, map race, class and toggles with pure functions in rxp-conditions.ts. To jump, open the matched catalog entry and select the visible step whose step.idx equals numbering[step-1]. Put the "Jump to live step" button in the existing timeline bar and hide it when the coach isn't running. Toggles otherwise default from config.json companion.rxp.* (the coach's defaults: SSF on). Note: the user's relayed request (the Explorer on its own page in the left rail, with no second scroll bar) was already built in cf08688. Live sync goes beyond that request.

### Gaps and risks

- Scope: the user's relayed request is to give the Explorer its own place in the left rail, contained like the Coach page, with no second scroll bar. HEAD already does that (commit cf08688; REFACTOR-CONTRACTS.md 'the Explorer is its own page'). Live sync is not in that request, so confirm with the user before building it. If it is built, put any new control in an existing row (the timeline bar) so the page stays tight and gains no scrolling.
- Hardcore and dungeon settings are not on the strip; page X carries only ss and gq. Live sync can set SSF and Group quests from the game, but Hardcore can only come from config.json companion.rxp.hardcore, which is what the coach numbers by too. A Hardcore player who never set it in Settings gets wrong numbers in both places. Fixing that needs an addon page X key (hc=, and possibly dungeons): a strip change (contract 1, strip_spec.py), a Glide.toc version bump and an addon golden re-record.
- The Explorer's numbering differs from RestedXP's on 14 of 80 free guides (and condition words such as 'sod'/'skip' are read differently). Mapping live step N onto the Explorer's own step number would land up to 18 steps off, so the jump must use Python's rxp_numbering. Porting step_shown, step_flags and guide_dungeons to TypeScript would make a second copy of a rule that views.py says is shared ('one rule for everyone') and would need a permanent parity test.
- guide_detail drops cond_flags and the guide header, so the renderer cannot compute the exact numbering itself. The Explorer also has no Dungeons toggle, yet RestedXP's default (dungeons off) hides dungeon steps.
- Only guides added in the app (imports.json) and RestedXP's free guides are in the catalog. A guide loaded in game another way (RestedXP's own import window) cannot be synced, even though kb.db holds its text (tools/build_db.py reads RestedXP's SavedVariables). The Explorer would need a kb.db-backed source to cover those.
- status() lists a bought guide only under its first #group (guide_meta). For a bought guide filed under both factions, a Horde character's guide_group won't match, so the resolver needs the name-plus-faction fallback.
- Version drift: if the game runs a different text than the Explorer reads (RestedXP updated mid-session, or a string re-added), numbering can be off. Without S0 (steps) on the light stream there is no cross-check. Adding steps costs about 700 B/s; the engine's ±6 S0-quest fallback (resolve.py _step_idx) could then be mirrored.
- When the coach isn't running there is no live data at all. The coach screen's iframe is cross-origin and can't be read. The only offline source is RestedXP's SavedVariables, written at logout or /reload, and the RXPCData field names for the current guide are unverified.
- In simulator runs the live guide is '1-14 Zephras Isle', which matches no current free guide, so the button must handle an unresolved guide ('<guide> isn't in your guides here') rather than failing silently.
- A numbering call spawns Python: 0.36 s here, likely about 1 s on Windows with a large glide.db if it goes through guide_detail. Cache per (guide, race, class, toggles) and render the TypeScript rule meanwhile. Use a dedicated command that skips the glide.db name lookups and map bounds.
- Page text cut mid-UTF-8 character arrives with a trailing U+FFFD (protocol.py decodes with 'replace'), while page_text drops the broken character. Strip trailing U+FFFD before comparing names; current names are ASCII, so this is theoretical today.
- A new IPC channel changes tests/golden/snapshots/desktop.json, so `golden.py record desktop` is needed, alone, with a recorded-change bullet. An optional third argument on addon:rxpImports avoids that but mixes return types.

## Knowledge-base data (quest chains, objectives, prep items, time/XP/hazards)

All the knowledge-base data the Explorer needs already exists in kb.db, and nothing in the kb schema has to change. Quest pickup, objective and turn-in steps come from the parsed actions (accept/turnin/complete/collect with quest_id). Follow-up quests come from quest.next_quest and quest_prereq (pre = required, pregroup = any one of the group), and simulate.unreachable() already answers "skipping X loses Y" the same way the coach does. Map targets for objectives come from quest_objective, then creature_spawn, gameobject_spawn, creature_drop/object_loot and quest_poi. The prep list can be built from the parser's verbs (.buy, .collect on a #ah or vendor step, .bankwithdraw/.bankdeposit), with names and vendors from item and vendor_item. Time, XP, level and risk per step come from step_brief, keyed by (guide_key, idx, UPPERCASE class). An Explorer guide maps to kb's rxp_guide.key as parse_guide()['key'], i.e. 'first #group (minus the << condition)||#name'. That matches tools/build_db.py's key for all 80 seed free guides, and the multi-faction Zephras guides use the (A) group. Step idx matches kb's rxp_step.idx only when the texts are identical and the guide has no #include. The kb loader expands #include and renumbers, but guide_detail does not. Nine free guide files use #include: the dungeon variants show as one empty step in the Explorer but have 71–847 steps in kb, and '14-16 Darkshore' drifts from step 166 (236 vs 340 steps). The guide text can also differ, because glide.db keeps the newest copy of each guide (import, RestedXP's own cache, or the addon file). And kb can predate the guide entirely: the refresh lands as data/kb.next-<t>.db, which rxp_import never reads. Recommended approach: (1) guide_detail expands includes over the same corpus the knowledge build uses, so the idx numbers are identical by construction. (2) Add one new engine command, `rxp_import.py facts <id> <index> [CLASS] [RACE]`, served by a new pure-read module knowledge/api/explorer.py (the R17 rule says the engine reads kb.db only through knowledge.api). It returns quests, step targets, hazards, prep items and the step_brief plan, together with how well kb matched the guide: exact, aligned (difflib over step texts) or none. It runs in a background subprocess in well under a second. Without kb it still answers everything the parse alone gives (quest step highlighting, prep list).

### Gaps and risks

- Include expansion: guide_detail never expands #include, so 9 free guide files (all dungeon variants plus Alliance-11-20 Darkshore) show 1–2 empty steps or shifted numbering, and idx cannot be joined to kb rxp_step / step_brief from the first include onward. This is a real Explorer bug as well as a join blocker.
- Version drift: kb's rxp_guide.text for a key can come from another source or version (RestedXP's cache, an installed addon file newer than the seed, an older import). Joining by idx without first checking the text silently attaches the wrong step's time, XP and risk.
- Stale kb: when the guide was added after the last build there is no rxp_guide row, so no step_brief, rxp_step_no or plan. The refresh build sits in data/kb.next-<t>.db until the next engine start, and rxp_import reads only data/kb.db, so even a finished refresh is invisible to the Explorer.
- Briefs exist only per (class, analysed race) under BRIEF_RXP settings. Choosing another race, or playing with solo self-found, group quests or dungeons set differently, changes which steps exist and RestedXP's numbering; the plan is then approximate, and steps hidden for that class have no row.
- Objective order risk: quest_objective.idx comes from the winning source's order (wdb > obs > fg). fg lists kill, item, object in that order, so an fg-only quest's `.complete q,N` may point at the wrong objective. Prefer drop_from and item matches for `.collect`, and the step's own .mob units.
- Kill-target spawns cover a whole zone (hundreds of points); without clipping to the step's waypoints and loop points (about 250–300 yd), the map and the JSON payload blow up.
- item.name is NULL for many vendor and AH items (fg_items holds about 3.6k mostly quest items). Names must fall back to the .collect comment ('Collect Gladius', 'Stiletto (1)' need cleaning) or to the bracketed text.
- Guide-independent facts (quests, objectives, spawns, hazards, vendors) need kb.db, which exists only on the player's PC; this container has none, so every test needs a small fixture kb.db.
- A conditional prep item (`<< Priest/Druid`, class-only step) must carry its step and action condition text so the renderer's conditionApplies() can filter it; otherwise every class sees every purchase.
- guide_detail drops args.obj, args.quest_ids and args.items, so the renderer alone cannot link `.complete q,N` to an objective or highlight quests named only by a multi-quest verb.
- Names come from glide.db (quests, entities), not kb.db; once glide.db is pruned or absent, quest badges lose their names.
- Windows: the short-lived rxp_import holds kb.db open read-only; promote/install fall back to .new.db if they collide with it. The risk is low, but the connection should close before the process exits.

### Recommended steps

1. One parse path for guide and facts. In companion/rxp_import.py add _parsed_guide(root, iid, index, wow_dir, kb) and use it in guide_detail. (a) Build a corpus index without full parsing: for free guides, free_guides() → per text key = parse_guide-style first-group||name via guide_meta(); for imports, load_store()['imports'][*]['guides'] (group, name). (b) Parse the selected text with parse_guide(text, map_ids=...), where map_ids = {name: ui_map} from kb `SELECT ui_map, name, parent FROM map ORDER BY parent = 0, ui_map` (setdefault per name, exactly as knowledge/rxp_parser/loader.py:24-26 does with glide.db maps). (c) Collect the #include targets of its steps with includes._include_entry and _include_target. Parse each target, recursively, and also every corpus guide with the same name, so that resolving by name sees the same candidates as the full build. Call expand_includes({key: parsed, ...}) and take the selected guide's steps. (d) Run place_world_points afterwards. Steps then carry flags.included_from, included_idx and include_note: pass them through, and show 'from <guide>' in the UI.
2. Choose the kb: newest_kb(root) returns the newest by mtime of data/kb.db and companion/kb_refresh.staged(os.path.join(root, 'data'), 'kb'). Open it with knowledge.store.open_ro(path), and refuse a kb whose store.schema_version(kb) != store.SCHEMA_VERSION (report the reason). Use it for map_bounds as well, replacing the hard-coded data/kb.db path in _map_bounds.
3. New pure-read module knowledge/api/explorer.py: guide_facts(kb, guide, cls=None, race=None, faction=None) -> dict. kb may be None; the function never raises and fills what it can. guide = the expanded parse from step 1.
4. Kb match, inside guide_facts. Look up the key: `SELECT key, text FROM rxp_guide WHERE key=?` with guide['key']; else `SELECT key FROM rxp_guide WHERE text=?`; else `SELECT key, text FROM rxp_guide WHERE name=?`. Then `SELECT idx, text FROM rxp_step WHERE guide_key=? ORDER BY idx`. match='exact' when the kb step-text list equals [s['text'] for s in guide['steps']] (same parser, same field); idx_map is then the identity. Otherwise run difflib.SequenceMatcher(None, mine, theirs, autojunk=False) over signatures text + '|' + the accept/turnin/complete quest ids and build idx_map from get_matching_blocks(): match='aligned'. No row: match='none', with a note that times and levels arrive after the next knowledge refresh. Report kb meta 'built' and `SELECT class, race, start_level, end_level, time_s, xp_total FROM guide_brief WHERE guide_key=?`.
5. Quests block (works without kb). qids = every action quest_id plus args.quest_ids. steps[q] = {accept:[idx], objective:[idx], turnin:[idx], gate:[idx]}, using the verb roles listed under Facts. With kb: `SELECT id, name, level, min_level, xp, xp_src, type, suggested_group, next_quest, removed, hidden FROM quest WHERE id IN (...)`; `SELECT quest_id, pre_id, kind FROM quest_prereq WHERE quest_id IN (...) OR pre_id IN (...)`; `SELECT id FROM quest WHERE next_quest IN (...)` (previous quest in the chain); `SELECT c.quest_id, c.chain_id, c.pos, (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM quest_chain c2 WHERE c2.chain_id=c.chain_id) n FROM quest_chain c WHERE c.quest_id IN (...)`; `SELECT quest_id, role, kind, entity_id, name, ui_map, x, y, pos_src FROM quest_giver WHERE quest_id IN (...) ORDER BY rowid`. lost_if_skipped[q] = simulate.unreachable(get_data(kb), q, scope) - {q}, where scope = this guide's accept and turn-in quest ids ∪ `SELECT DISTINCT quest_id FROM rxp_action WHERE guide_key IN (<next keys from guide['next_list'] as group||name>) AND verb IN ('accept','turnin')`. For each lost quest give its name and accept idx here, or 'next guide'. Also list exclusive (kind 'exclusive') and breadcrumb rows, and decay_at_level = model.decay_level(xp, level). Chunk IN lists to 900 parameters or fewer.
6. Per-step targets for the map, only on steps with objective work. From `.complete q,obj`: `SELECT quest_id, idx, type, target_kind, target_id, amount, text, drop_from_kind, drop_from_id FROM quest_objective WHERE quest_id IN (...)`, matched on idx = args.obj. From `.collect item,qty,q`: the objective with target_kind='item' AND target_id=item, else `SELECT creature_id, item_id, rate FROM creature_drop WHERE item_id IN (...)` and `SELECT object_id, item_id, rate FROM object_loot WHERE item_id IN (...)`. From .mob/.unitscan units: the id, or `SELECT id FROM creature WHERE lower(name)=lower(?)` preferring one that spawns on the step map. Points: `SELECT creature_id, x, y, n FROM creature_spawn WHERE creature_id IN (...) AND ui_map=? AND x IS NOT NULL` and `SELECT object_id, x, y FROM gameobject_spawn WHERE object_id IN (...) AND ui_map=? AND x IS NOT NULL`. Keep the points within 300 yd of the step's gotos, waypoints and loop points, measuring yards with `SELECT ui_map, width_yd, height_yd FROM map WHERE ui_map IN (...)`; if none fall inside, keep the nearest cluster. Thin to a 1% grid and cap at 40 points per target and 6 targets per step. Add `SELECT quest_id, role, ui_map, x, y, n FROM quest_poi WHERE quest_id IN (...) AND x IS NOT NULL`, and names from `SELECT id, name, min_level, max_level, rank, friendly_to FROM creature WHERE id IN (...)` and `SELECT id, name FROM gameobject WHERE id IN (...)`. Shape: {kind npc|object, id, name, level, rank, role kill|loot|use|talk, quest, obj, ui_map, points:[[x,y]]}.
7. Hazards per step. When there is a matching brief, use justification.risks plus kill targets with rank 1, 2 or 3. Otherwise, for steps with a position, use briefs._risks_near(get_data(kb), (ui_map, x, y), 150, faction, guide level_min), the same rule as the brief. Add notes from `SELECT name, creature_id, min_level, max_level, classification, movement, notes FROM creature_note WHERE creature_id IN (...) OR (creature_id IS NULL AND name IN (...))`, plus quest-level flags: type in (elite, group, dungeon, escort, timed), suggested_group, removed or hidden.
8. Prep list (works without kb). Walk the steps in order. Classify: .buy/.buyAll/.buyUntilBroke → 'buy'; .collect on a step with flags.ah → 'ah'; .collect where the step has .vendor or 'buy'/'purchase' in the step or action text (the analyzer's rule) → 'buy'; .bankwithdraw → 'bank_out' (args.items); .bankdeposit → 'bank_in'. Leave .itemcount out (a gate). ## Map imagery and coordinates

The user's main ask is already in place: commit cf08688 gave the Explorer its own rail entry (index.html:16, `data-page="guides"`). The page is sized to the window, the map stays 3:2 at the largest size that fits (app.css:507-552), and only the step list scrolls. Zoom and pan (1-8x, wheel, drag, Follow) came in 2503c00, with no fixed-size or side-scrolling parts in the map column. In my area the map images are fine. tools/extract_maps.py writes each zone, continent, dungeon and micro map as data/gamedata/maps/<uiMapID>.png at the art layer's size, which the code treats as 1002x668 (aspect 1.5) everywhere: the map_bounds loader, areas.json, geo.AreaRaster and the coach's own map. That matches the Explorer's 1002x668 viewBox, so pins are not misplaced. The Explorer still has four problems. (1) It uses `object-fit: cover`, while the coach uses `fill`; if a map's art were ever not 3:2 it would be cropped and the pins would shift. (2) Its distance readout assumes every zone is 3500x2400 yd and its arrow angle uses percent rather than yards, so both are wrong. (3) It has no geometry linking one zone to another or to the continent, so legs that cross zones, and flights, draw nothing. (4) The main process base64-encodes every zone PNG on every guide open (several MB per guide, cached for good in the renderer), even when the engine is running and the renderer loads the image over HTTP anyway. Recommended approach: 1) serve map images through a small privileged `glide://map/<id>.png` protocol in the main process, and fill `mapImages` with those URLs instead of data: URLs. 2) Return each zone's size in yards and its rectangle on its continent from `guide_detail`; this is one read-only kb.db query on map plus map_bounds. 3) Put the coordinate math in a small TypeScript module that can be tested on its own (yards, bearing, zone to continent, zone to zone, clipping), and use it for cross-zone legs and a continent view. 4) Compute flight curves from TaxiNet's CSVs in `guide_detail`. 5) Get real walking paths from a new batch command, `rxp_import.py walks`, that caches its results and calls walk_route outside the engine. Going through the engine would evict the coach's one cached continent graph. Do not share runtime code with the coach screen's map (plain JS served by the engine inside an iframe, versus compiled TS loaded from file://). Share its route data shape (WalkSegment) and its look instead.

### Gaps and risks

- No data/gamedata, data/kb.db or data/db2 in this container: the 1002x668 size of every PNG, its file size (about 1.5-2.5 MB, estimated) and the map_bounds coverage of the Forever-only maps (2482, 2521, 2524, 2548, 2652) can't be checked here. Verify on the player's PC by reading the IHDR of each PNG (bytes 16-24) and running SELECT ui_map, src FROM map_bounds.
- Risk, unverified: .rxpb-map-layer has will-change: transform. Chromium may keep a will-change layer rastered at its first scale, so pins, lines and labels in the SVG could look soft at 4-8x zoom. Check at 2560x1440; if they blur, drop will-change or set it only while dragging. The 1002 px map bitmap pixelates past about 3x zoom whatever is done (the game's own resolution).
- object-fit: cover (app.css:550) is safe only while every image is exactly 3:2. It is a silent misplacement waiting to happen; fill (as the coach map does) has no downside at 3:2.
- Distance and bearing in the arrow readout are wrong today (fixed 35/24 yd per %, bearing computed in percent). A regression test should come with the fix (CLAUDE.md).
- Cross-zone legs, flights, hearths and boat or zeppelin legs draw nothing: legs are searched only within the zone shown (rxp-browser.ts:362-371), and the Explorer has no zone-to-continent transform.
- Real walking paths are expensive. A NavGraph load is about 40-70 MB, decompressed per process (an estimated 1-3 s), and an A* search per leg, times 100-300 legs per guide, adds up to tens of seconds of CPU. They must be windowed (the active leg plus the next few), cached on disk, and run at below-normal priority (kb_refresh.py uses BELOW_NORMAL creationflags; engine.pythonJson has no priority option yet). Without data/gamedata/nav or terrain and kb.db they are unavailable: keep the straight dashed leg.
- Asking the running engine for walks (a new /walk route) would thrash nav.load_graph's single-continent cache whenever the Explorer's guide is on the other continent from the player. If ever added, that route must refuse other continents.
- Memory: the renderer's guide cache (rxp-browser.ts:41) never evicts and today holds base64 copies of every zone image for every guide opened.
- Continents: the world map (947) is never extracted, so legs between continents (boats, zeppelins) have no map; show a continent switch and a text note instead.
- The seed in the repo has no 'maps' (the continent 'kids' rectangles), so map.continent and propagated bounds depend on the player's seed and DB2. The rectangles must come from map_bounds of both the zone and the continent, and are null when either is missing.
- Contract records needed: mapImages content (contract 4), any new IPC channel (4), a new rxp_import.py subcommand (7), the CSP/protocol addition (player-visible behaviour, no text change), plus re-recording the golden desktop probe if main-module exports change.
